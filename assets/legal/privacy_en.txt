SOCKSICLE DESKTOP - PRIVACY POLICY
=============================================================
Last Updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Socksicle Desktop application
("the Application", "we", "us", or "our").

We respect user privacy. Socksicle Desktop is architected from the ground
up as an autonomous client-side application operating under a strict
no-logs policy.


1. NO-LOGS POLICY (NETWORK TRAFFIC)
-------------------------------------------------------------
- We do NOT inspect, collect, monitor, record, store, or share your
  network traffic, DNS requests, visited domains, data payloads, or
  connection metadata.
- When connected (either via local SOCKS5 proxy or full TUN virtual adapter),
  all traffic is processed locally on your device by the chosen routing core
  (sing-box or Xray) and routed directly to the remote proxy server configured
  by you or your subscription provider.
- We do not own, operate, or route traffic through intermediary proxy servers.


2. DATA STORED LOCALLY ON YOUR COMPUTER
-------------------------------------------------------------
All data required for the operation of the Application resides exclusively
in your user-specific local application directory (%APPDATA%/socksicle on
Windows or ~/.config/socksicle on Linux):
- Proxy server profiles and configurations (VLESS, VMess, Shadowsocks,
  Hysteria 2, Trojan, TUIC, etc.).
- Subscription URLs and cached node lists.
- Interface preferences (theme mode, accent color, language, tray behavior,
  and launch settings).
- Custom routing rules, direct/bypass lists, and DNS server settings.
- Diagnostic logs of proxy cores (if logging is explicitly enabled by you).

This data is strictly private and is never uploaded to our servers or to
any third party.


3. SUBSCRIPTION UPDATES AND EXTERNAL SERVERS
-------------------------------------------------------------
- When you add or refresh a subscription, the Application performs direct
  HTTP/HTTPS requests to the third-party endpoint providing your subscription.
- When connecting to a proxy node, the network connection is established
  directly between your computer and the target proxy server.
- The developers of Socksicle Desktop have no access to, affiliation with,
  or responsibility for the privacy practices and logging policies of
  third-party subscription providers or server operators.


4. LOCAL HARDWARE IDENTIFIER (HWID / INSTALLATION ID)
-------------------------------------------------------------
- The Application may compute a local hardware identifier (HWID) based on
  system identifiers (such as motherboard serial or machine-id) solely for
  internal configuration indexing or unlocking developer tools.
- This identifier is kept strictly on your local machine and is never linked
  to your personal identity, transmitted over the network, or used for tracking.


5. THIRD-PARTY ANALYTICS AND TELEMETRY
-------------------------------------------------------------
- Socksicle Desktop contains NO built-in analytics frameworks, telemetry,
  advertising SDKs, or background metric collectors.
- The Application does not monitor your activity across other applications
  or websites.


6. OPERATING SYSTEM PERMISSIONS AND NETWORK INTERFACES
-------------------------------------------------------------
The Application requests only the permissions necessary for the selected mode:
- Local Proxy Mode (SOCKS5 / HTTP): Runs within standard user-space permissions
  without requiring elevated administrative privileges.
- TUN Virtual Adapter Mode: Interacts with the system network stack to capture
  and route system-wide traffic:
  * On Windows: Uses the Wintun driver (wintun.dll), requiring administrator
    privileges (User Account Control / UAC prompt).
  * On Linux: Creates a virtual TUN interface (/dev/net/tun), requiring root
    privileges or PolicyKit authentication (pkexec or CAP_NET_ADMIN).
- Autostart: When enabled in Settings, registers a standard user-level startup entry
  in the Windows Registry (HKCU) or a desktop entry in ~/.config/autostart on Linux.


7. CRASH REPORTS AND DIAGNOSTIC LOGS
-------------------------------------------------------------
- Diagnostic information and core logs are kept solely in local storage.
- Crash dumps and logs are NEVER transmitted automatically.
- Any sharing of logs with developers (via GitHub Issues or community channels)
  is performed exclusively through manual, voluntary action by the user.


8. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY
-------------------------------------------------------------
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be
published in the official project repository with the revision date.
