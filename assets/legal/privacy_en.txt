SOCKSICLE - PRIVACY POLICY
=============================================================
Last Updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Socksicle application
("the Application", "we", "us", or "our").

We take user privacy seriously. Socksicle is designed from the ground up as
a client-side tool with a strict no-logs approach.


1. NO-LOGS POLICY (INTERNET TRAFFIC)
-------------------------------------------------------------
- We do NOT inspect, collect, monitor, record, store, or sell your internet
  traffic, DNS requests, visited websites, data payloads, or connection metadata.
- When the VPN service is active, all network traffic is processed locally on
  your device and forwarded directly to the remote server configurations or
  proxies specified by you or your subscription provider.
- We operate no centralized intermediary servers that intercept your traffic.


2. DATA STORED LOCALLY ON YOUR DEVICE
-------------------------------------------------------------
All data required for the operation of the Application is stored solely on
your device inside the Application's private storage sandbox:
- Proxy server profiles and configurations (VLESS, Hysteria2, Shadowsocks, etc.).
- Subscription URLs and fetched server lists.
- User interface preferences (chosen theme, sorting mode, language).
- Routing rules, per-app proxy lists, and bypass settings.
- Diagnostic logs, if enabled by you for troubleshooting.

This information is never transmitted to our servers or any third-party
analytics providers.


3. SUBSCRIPTION UPDATES AND EXTERNAL SERVERS
-------------------------------------------------------------
- When you add a subscription URL or enable automatic subscription updates,
  the Application makes standard HTTP/HTTPS requests directly to the third-party
  provider hosting your subscription.
- When connecting to a proxy server, the connection is established directly
  between your device and the target server.
- We have no access to, control over, or responsibility for the privacy practices,
  logs, or policies of third-party subscription providers or server operators.


4. DEVICE IDENTIFIERS AND LOCAL HARDWARE ID (HWID)
-------------------------------------------------------------
- The Application may compute a local hardware identifier (HWID) on your
  device solely for local configuration management or developer feature unlock.
- This identifier is kept strictly on your device and is not linked to your
  personal identity or transmitted to third-party advertising or tracking networks.


5. THIRD-PARTY SDKs AND ANALYTICS
-------------------------------------------------------------
- Socksicle does NOT contain third-party advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob).
- Socksicle does NOT contain third-party behavioral analytics or tracking SDKs
  (such as Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, AppsFlyer, etc.).
- The Application does not track your activity across third-party apps or websites.


6. ANDROID SYSTEM PERMISSIONS
-------------------------------------------------------------
The Application requests only the minimal permissions required for VPN and
proxy functionality:
- android.permission.BIND_VPN_SERVICE: Required to create a local virtual network
  interface (TUN) for routing traffic through the selected proxy core.
- android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SYSTEM_EXEMPTED:
  Required by Android to maintain continuous proxy connections in the background
  without being prematurely terminated by the operating system.
- android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used strictly to show the ongoing VPN
  connection status and quick-disconnect controls in the Android status bar.


7. CRASH REPORTS AND USER-INITIATED LOGS
-------------------------------------------------------------
- If an error occurs, diagnostic information is logged only into the local memory
  or local storage of the device.
- Crash reports and application logs are NEVER uploaded automatically.
- Any bug report or log file shared by you (via GitHub Issues or Telegram) is
  transmitted solely upon your explicit manual action.


8. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY
-------------------------------------------------------------
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be
reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document
and publishing the revised policy in the official repository.
